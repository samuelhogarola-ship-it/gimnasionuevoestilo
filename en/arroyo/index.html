The gym

A place to train. For real.

The Arroyo de la Miel location follows the Nuevo Estilo line: a place to train with consistency, personal attention and criteria.

It is located in Conjunto Pueblo Sol, Calle Mercurio, Bloque 6, in Benalmadena, with training room, instructor-led activities and practical services for everyday training.

Questions

Before you visit.

Quick answers so the page is ready when details are confirmed.

How can I sign up?
Call +34 951 21 10 28 or ask directly at reception.
Is there a commitment period?
No. There is no commitment period: pay for the option you are going to use according to your training rhythm.
Can I visit before signing up?
Yes. You can visit Conjunto Pueblo Sol, Calle Mercurio, Bloque 6, and ask about current options.
Where can I check the updated hours?
Monday to Friday from 6:30 to 23:00, and Saturdays and Sundays from 9:00 to 14:00.
Is there parking?
The location is in Conjunto Pueblo Sol, Arroyo de la Miel. Check Google Maps before coming to plan your route.
