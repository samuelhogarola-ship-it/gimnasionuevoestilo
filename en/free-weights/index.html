Free weights for proper training.

Free weights make it easy to adjust load, technique and progression. The area includes dumbbells, bars and bench press stations: 2 flat, 2 incline and 1 decline bench.

Before you visit

Quick questions.

For specific equipment, busy times or current prices, call reception.

Can beginners use free weights?
Yes, as long as load and technique are adapted to your level. Ask the team if you are unsure.
How much does access cost?
The published monthly price is 40€ and the 3-month option is 100€. There is no commitment period: pay for what you are going to train, with other options confirmed at reception.
