Before you visit

Quick questions.

For current availability or a specific training question, call reception before you come in.

Can I train for one day?
Yes. A one-day pass is published at 5€. See prices
Is the gym suitable for visitors staying in Fuengirola?
Yes. Day, one-week and two-week passes are available for short stays.
Do you have free weights and machines?
Yes. The strength room includes free weights, bars, benches, machines and cables, with cardio and instructor-led activities as well.
Is Nuevo Estilo good for bodybuilding or serious strength training?
The gym has a strength-focused atmosphere and is led by Paco Mula, bodybuilding competitor and coach.
