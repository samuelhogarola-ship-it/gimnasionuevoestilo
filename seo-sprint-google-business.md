- Machines and cables: 5 photos, bright and uncrowded where possible.
- Cardio and spinning: 4 to 5 photos.
- Classes and reception: 3 to 4 photos.
- Real training atmosphere: 3 to 4 photos, only with permission from anyone identifiable.

Upload original, well-lit photos during the month. Avoid collages, graphics with promotional text, heavily filtered images and duplicates.

## Review request

Ask after a positive interaction, in person or by personal message. Do not offer incentives or suggest wording.

> Thanks for training with us. If your visit went well, an honest Google review helps other people in Fuengirola find the gym. You can mention what was useful about your visit, in your own words.

Prioritise international and bilingual members naturally, but never filter for positive reviews.
